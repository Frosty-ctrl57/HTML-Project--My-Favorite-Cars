Welcome to my favourite Cars

=======

The R34’s body isn’t one of the most aerodynamic out there, same case with the R35, but whilst this was the car to be last adorned with both the Skyline and GT-R badges, it is one of the best cars to ever have left Japan. It is aggressive (Just look at those headlights), it has that “boxy” shape to it, while it is still pretty fast, the shape of it is so perfect that only Nismo itself could improve it and make it look better, that’s why there are many bodykits that tried to improve the looks but failed, except for the Top Secret’s one and the ones done especially for motorsport. Another huge thing about it is the spoiler, It isn’t that big, nor that small, contrary to many spoilers, this one has all the rounded edges that contribute to the general shape of the car. The headlights are also very distinctive, just by seeing the two red assymetric circles and the exhaust badge, you know that you’re in for a good view
